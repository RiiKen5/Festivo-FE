# Content Strategy

Content must follow **audience → problem → discovery → product → conversion**. No generic blog.

## What content is worth making

### 1. Vendor-facing (supply acquisition), highest priority

| Asset | Problem it answers | Where it's distributed | Converts to |
|---|---|---|---|
| "How to stop losing advances and no-shows" one-pager + 60s video | Chasing payments, ghosted quotes | DMs to vendors, home-baker WhatsApp/Facebook groups (with admin permission) | `/for-vendors` → listing |
| Outreach scripts (Instagram DM, WhatsApp, phone) | — | Founder outreach | Onboarding call |
| "Your Festivo link" guide (put it in your bio, send it with quotes) | How to use Festivo with existing customers | Sent after onboarding | Vendor-shared bookings |
| Vendor spotlights (with permission, real work photos) | Social proof for other vendors and organizers | Instagram, vendor pages | Listings + bookings |

### 2. Organizer-facing (demand), after supply

| Asset | Search/discovery | Converts to |
|---|---|---|
| Category × area pages with real vendors (see SEO) | High-intent local search | Booking request |
| "Birthday at home: what it costs in Pune" (real price ranges from listings) | Problem-aware search, Instagram | Vendor list |
| Kids' birthday checklist (matches the in-app checklist) | Search, parent WhatsApp groups | Create event |

### 3. Host-facing (loop)

| Asset | Converts to |
|---|---|
| "Run your society's Diwali event: RSVPs, tickets, check-in" one-pager | Event creation by committees |
| Template events (birthday, farewell, society festival) inside the product | Faster event setup |

## Repeatable engine (only for Instagram, where vendors and organizers both are)

One real event → before/after photos from the vendor (with consent) → Instagram reel + carousel → vendor reposts → vendor's Festivo link in caption → Festivo vendor page.

Cadence: 2–3 posts/week **only from real events**. No stock imagery, no invented testimonials.

## Not now

Long-form SEO articles, newsletters, LinkedIn, X, YouTube channels. Wrong audience or premature without supply.
