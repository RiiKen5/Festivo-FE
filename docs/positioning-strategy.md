# Positioning Strategy

## Framework

| | |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Booking marketplace for small personal events (not weddings) |
| **Target** | People planning birthdays, anniversaries and house parties in their city, and the home/micro vendors who serve them |
| **Problem** | Booking small-event vendors runs on DMs and risky advances; vendors chase payments and lose orders to no-shows |
| **Differentiator** | The money is held until the job is done, and vendors set their own final price. No directory or chat app can offer that. |
| **Outcome** | Organizers book with confidence; vendors get customers who have already paid |

## Candidate value propositions

| # | Proposition | Evidence / fit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "Book the people who make your event happen. Your money is held until the job is done." | Directly answers the organizer's trust problem; true today; one sentence | **Strongest for organizers** |
| B | "Customers who pay before the event, not after." | Speaks to the vendor's #1 pain (chasing advances, no-shows) | **Strongest for vendors** |
| C | "Plan your whole party in one place: vendors, invites, tickets." | True, but "all-in-one" is generic and weak against focused tools | Supporting line only |
| D | "Verified vendors near you." | Directories claim the same; verification is manual and small today | Don't lead with it |
| E | "Cheaper than booking directly." | Not true (listed price is the vendor's price) | **Never use** |

Pick **A** for demand and **B** for supply. They are the same mechanism (held payment) seen from both sides, which keeps messaging consistent.

## Messaging hierarchy

**Organizers**
1. Your advance is safe until the job is done.
2. See real prices and real reviews (from completed bookings only).
3. The vendor sends a final quote, so no haggling in DMs.
4. Invite guests and sell tickets from the same place.

**Vendors**
1. Customers pay before the event; you're paid after it, to UPI or bank.
2. Free to list; you set your final price.
3. Fewer ghosted quotes; every request comes with date, event and requirements.
4. Reviews only from real customers, so your track record is yours.

**Hosts**
1. One link for the invite, RSVP and ticket.
2. Private events stay invite-only.
3. Check people in with a code; automatic refunds if you cancel.

## Proof we can use (truthful)

- How it works, with screenshots of real flows.
- The published cancellation & refund policy.
- "Verified by Festivo" badge (once vendors are verified).
- Later: real numbers (bookings completed, vendors onboarded) only when they exist and are accurate.

## Words to use / avoid

- Use: *held*, *protected until delivery*, *final price*, *real reviews*, *local*.
- Avoid: *guaranteed*, *insured*, *best*, *cheapest*, *thousands of*, *verified* (unless the specific vendor is).
