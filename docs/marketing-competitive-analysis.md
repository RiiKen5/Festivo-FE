# Competitive Analysis

_Research: October 2026 web search plus public knowledge of these brands. Treat traction claims as the companies' own; verify pricing on their sites before using in comparisons._

## Landscape

| Group | Examples | What they do well | Where they're vulnerable (Festivo's opening) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wedding marketplaces | WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga (founded 2013), WeddingWire India, ShaadiRoot, newer entrants like BookMyHalwai | Huge vendor catalogues, strong SEO for "wedding photographers in X", inspiration content | Built for weddings: lead-gen (vendor pays for leads), no protected payment, small birthdays/house parties are an afterthought |
| Managed celebration services | CherishX (Delhi, 2015; says 15+ cities incl. Pune; decor from about ₹499, 2-hour setups from about ₹1,399), local "decor at home" brands | Fixed packages, simple checkout, brand-delivered quality, ads + Instagram | Fixed catalogue (no local independent vendors, no quotes); they *are* the vendor, so home decorators are competitors to them, not partners |
| Directories / lead-gen | JustDial, Sulekha, Google Maps, IndiaMART | Massive search presence and local coverage | No transaction, no payment protection, vendors complain about paying for low-quality leads; organizers still DM and pay advances |
| Instagram + WhatsApp | (the real incumbent) | Free, visual, where vendors already are, trust via followers | No protection, no structured requests, advances in notebooks, discovery depends on the algorithm |
| Cake/gift brands | FNP, Bakingo, Winni, Swiggy/Zomato bakery listings | Fast delivery, strong brands, ads | Standard products; custom-theme home bakers are exactly who they don't serve well |
| Ticketing / RSVP | Townscript (Pune; BookMyShow-owned), AllEvents, KonfHub, District, Luma, Google Forms | Mature ticketing, discovery feeds (AllEvents/District), polished RSVP (Luma) | None connect the host to vendors; private home events are poorly served |
| Home-baker tools | Order-management apps for bakers (e.g. Kamai, Cakenest, which charge subscriptions) | Help vendors organize orders | No demand side; vendor pays, gets no new customers |

## Where competitors are strong

- **SEO for wedding vendors** and **directory listings for "X near me"**: don't fight head-on.
- **Instagram** as the vendor storefront: don't try to replace it; plug into it.
- **Managed packages** (CherishX) for buyers who want zero decisions.

## Where they're weak (our openings)

1. **No one protects the organizer's advance** for small-event vendors. This is our wedge.
2. **Home/micro vendors are underserved**: lead-gen is expensive for them and managed brands compete with them.
3. **Ticketing tools ignore vendors**, and vendor platforms ignore guests. Festivo connects the event to both.
4. **Small celebrations are date-driven and local**: long-tail searches like "balloon decoration at home in Baner" are less contested than wedding keywords.

## Implications

- Position as *the safe way to book small-event vendors*, not as "another vendor directory".
- Recruit the **independent vendors** that managed brands compete with and directories overcharge.
- Make Festivo the **checkout link vendors share from Instagram/WhatsApp** (complement their storefront rather than replacing it).
- Don't spend on wedding keywords or broad "event management" ads.
